Accumulating documents, pages and conversations does not make an organization intelligent.

## From knowledge to the ability to act

The Enterprise Brain asks a different question: how does the organization retrieve, connect and mobilize its knowledge when a decision or action requires it?

## Measuring collective intelligence

The useful measures are capabilities: finding expertise, connecting information, onboarding new people and preserving knowledge when people leave.

The concept remains in OKF; this article is one publication built from it.
