Before automating a process, we first need to know how it really works — and whether it works at all.

## Observe before automating

Documented processes, described processes and actual behaviour can differ significantly. Without audit data, automation often encodes assumptions.

## From Source of Truth to Source of Proof

An organization needs more than authoritative information. It also needs evidence of what actually happened: who acted, when, with which identity, on which data and through which system.
