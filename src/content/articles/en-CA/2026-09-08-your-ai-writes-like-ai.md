We often talk about bad prompts. We talk less about the thing that changes output quality most: explaining how we want the system to work with us.

## Voice is context

Tone, sentence length, structures to avoid and representative examples are part of the useful context.

## Persistent instructions

The goal is not to discover a magic sentence. It is to build a coherent and reusable writing environment.

That practice belongs in OKF, while this article remains a reader-facing publication.
