I was wrong about Power Automate. For years, I often treated automation as something the solution creator should also build.

AI agents are making me reconsider that model.

## The agent orchestrates

The agent interprets intent, selects the next step and coordinates work.

## Automation executes

Power Automate remains the execution layer: deterministic, testable, observable and governable.

## The platform governs

Agent design, automation engineering and platform governance are different responsibilities.

The reusable knowledge behind that distinction lives in the OKF object, independently from this English publication.
